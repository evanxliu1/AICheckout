# Barclays conventions (expansion.v1)

Issuer-specific decisions for Barclays cards. They refine [general.md](general.md) and never contradict it. Seeded by the coordinator on 2026-10-02 from the verifier pass; confirmed, corrected and extended by the adjudicator on 2026-10-02.

- **Activation (general 7), confirmed and extended 2026-10-02.** Activation is `none` on every rule of a card whose terms enroll the cardholder in the partner program without a step of their own: "automatically enrolled" (Gap Inc. Encore family), "you consent to and will be enrolled" (Wyndham Rewards Earner family, Emirates Skywards, GM Rewards, Breeze), "a new … Account number will be assigned to you" (JetBlue family, Carnival, Frontier) and "are enrolled in the Upromise Program" (Upromise base rate). The anchor is the sentence part that states the enrollment, not the clipped "If the … number that you provided is found to be invalid" window.
- **Activation exceptions, 2026-10-02.** Lufthansa Miles & More stays `enroll-once`: its terms require a cardholder without a membership to enroll at miles-and-more.com to receive Miles. The Upromise 1.529% rule stays `enroll-once`: it needs a one-time link to an eligible College Savings Plan. Cards whose terms say nothing about enrollment (Luxury cards, AARP, Barnes & Noble, Capital Vacations, RCI) stay null.
- **GM Rewards (general 2), confirmed 2026-10-02.** Only the card-paid 7X counts (700); the extra 3 points GM "may" award to GM Rewards members are excluded and described in the product note.
- **Gap Inc. Encore family, confirmed and extended 2026-10-02.** Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic and Athleta share one terms capture. Labels use the terms' Mastercard-level rates (25 / 15 / 5 points per $1; payment-platform purchases 5) with an `ambiguous` issue for the World/World Elite rate (10). The product pages advertise 5X/3X/1X; the labels follow the terms and each card carries an `ambiguous` issue for the mismatch. `pointValueHundredthsOfCent` is 20 (general 14, final consistency pass 2026-10-02): Encore points are store-only points with a stated fixed value, "500 points = $1 Reward", on a co-branded card as on a store card. It replaces the earlier null under general 3. Each card also carries the `ambiguous` store-only issue anchored on "redeemable in the Encore Market … or on purchases at the Gap Inc. brands", and the value stays in the `closed-loop` product note.
- **JetBlue (general 4), confirmed 2026-10-02.** JetBlue and JetBlue Vacations purchases are separate `other` rules; TrueBlue Travel (truebluetravel.com) is `travel-portal`. The combined "3X/6X on JetBlue, JetBlue Vacations and TrueBlue Travel Purchases" heading is split into the three rules on all three cards.
- **Luxury cards point value (general 3), 2026-10-02.** Titanium 100, Black 150, Gold 200, from the terms' fixed statement-credit (and bank-deposit) redemption rate (1%, 1.5%, 2%). The 2% airfare redemption rate is travel-only and does not set the value.
- **Other point values, 2026-10-02.** RCI 100 (2,500 Rewards for a $25 statement credit). Barnes & Noble points redeem only as $25 B&N gift cards per 2,500 points: null, with a `closed-loop` product note. Under general 14 (final consistency pass 2026-10-02) the card also carries the `ambiguous` store-only issue anchored on the eGiftCard sentence; the point value stays null because gift cards are not point values. Travel-award values (Breeze "$400 value", TrueBlue pay-yourself-back) stay null.
- **Upromise, 2026-10-02.** Two `all-purchases` rules: 1.25% when no College Savings Plan is linked (125, the base) and 1.529% when linked (rateBps null, because 152.9 bps is not a whole number; the existing `out-of-scope` issue records why). The MyGiftCardsPlus gift-card benefit has no earn rate and is a merchant product note, not a rule.
- **Co-brand "up to" totals (general 2), 2026-10-02.** Frontier "up to 17x" → card 5X; Carnival "up to 6x" → card 3X; Breeze "up to 10X/4X" → card 5X/2X; the partner's share goes in the product note.
- **Issues (general 11), 2026-10-02.** Draft `missing` issues with no anchor, or anchored only on earning sentences that are already rules, redemption pointers or the merchant-coding boilerplate, are removed. The "Additional points may not be earned … mobile or digital wallet … third-party payment account" clause is boilerplate on every Barclays card and is not recorded per card.


## Overlay (Stage 2 M4)

Decisions for `../../catalog-overlay.json`, made by the issuer's overlay author on 2026-10-02 under the general overlay conventions (O1–O19), then checked by an independent verifier subagent and adjudicated by the M4 coordinator (agent-verified).

- **Payment-path clauses, 2026-10-02 (coordinator ruling on O10).** Every Barclays rewards section says bonus rewards "may not" be earned with a mobile or digital wallet, and that third-party payment accounts "will not earn additional" rewards. Under the clarified O10, hedged wording also counts. Every kept bonus rule (rate above the base) therefore gets `excludedPaymentPaths: [paypal, venmo, digital-wallet]`, anchored on that card's own wallet clause and third-party clause. The two anchors are about 10 words apart, so they do not form a run. The corpus still does not record the clause per card. Rules at the base rate (Breeze No Flex Fares and Other Breeze Bookings) get no exclusion. The Barnes & Noble rebate gets one after adjudication (W2).
- **Target/Walmart, 2026-10-02.** Grocery rules that exclude Target and Walmart (Breeze, Carnival, all three Wyndham cards) and the AARP Essential drug store rule get `excludedBrandIds: [target, walmart]`. The JetBlue terms only say these stores are "generally not classified" as grocery stores. The same exclusion is applied there as the conservative reading.
- **Gap Inc. Encore family, 2026-10-02.** The 25-point Family of Brands rule is brand-scoped to gap, old-navy, banana-republic and athleta. It excludes paypal, venmo, bnpl and digital-wallet: named payment platforms earn 5 points, third-party payment accounts do not qualify, and the wallet clause applies. The payment-platform rule (5 points) is brand-scoped to the same four brands with `requiredPaymentPaths: [paypal, bnpl]`. PayPal maps to paypal, and Afterpay and Klarna map to bnpl. TikTok has no path. Brand-store purchases outside the U.S. are held out. Apparel outside the brands is held out, in the U.S. and abroad ("no v3 category: clothing"). The World and World Elite 10-point rate is an added `all-purchases` rule gated on the new `gap-encore-card-level` gate. The labelled 5-point Mastercard-level base stays unconditional. Encore points get the four brands as redemption brands.
- **Barnes & Noble, 2026-10-02.** The 5% rebate is brand-scoped to the eight store names the terms list. bn.com is barnes-and-noble. After adjudication (W2) it excludes paypal, venmo and digital-wallet. The restaurant rule excludes the barnes-and-noble brand, because Café purchases do not count (W4). barnes-noble-points redeems only at barnes-and-noble (gift cards).
- **GM Rewards, 2026-10-02.** The 7X rule is brand-scoped to general-motors, siriusxm and onstar, since the terms list SiriusXM and OnStar transactions by name (W3). GM Rewards points get general-motors as their redemption brand: vehicle, GM Financial balance and GM experiences.
- **Lufthansa, 2026-10-02.** The base keeps its rate. Its activation goes from enroll-once to `unstated` (O6). The terms ask non-members to enroll, and no capture says enrollment is automatic. The 2X ticket rule keeps enroll-once and is brand-scoped to the nine integrated partner airlines the terms name.
- **Upromise, 2026-10-02.** The 1.529% linked-plan rule is held out under O6 because the rate is not a whole number of basis points. The 1.25% rule is the base, so the catalog understates accounts with a linked plan.
- **Co-brand partner rules, 2026-10-02.** Each partner rule is brand-scoped. JetBlue and JetBlue Vacations are separate brands. Breeze (the 1X No Flex Fares and Other Bookings rules; the bundle rules are held out under O19), Frontier, Emirates, Carnival Cruise Line, Capital Vacations and RCI each get one brand. Hotels by Wyndham uses the shared wyndham-hotels ID. Wyndham Destinations (Premier's "Vacation Club") is scoped to the four clubs it names. Generic travel rules are held out as "not at retail": Wyndham Plus and Premier travel, Emirates Travel Spends, RCI travel, AARP Travel airfare/hotel/car. AARP Essential medical is also held out as not at retail.
- **Capital Vacations "RCI, travel" rule, 2026-10-02.** One corpus rule covers RCI and generic travel. It is brand-scoped to rci, and the generic travel part is dropped, as it is not at retail.

### Verification and adjudication (2026-10-02)

The coordinator's resolution of each finding of the independent verifier subagent:

- W1: Breeze rules 1 (Nicer/Nicest Bundles 5X) and 2 (Nice Bundles 2X) are `rule-held-out` under O19. The fare bundle is a purchase-level condition that the Breeze brand scope cannot separate, so keeping the rules would pay 5X on every Breeze purchase. Hints 0 and 1 are now `noted`. Rules 3 and 4 (1X) stay brand-scoped.
- W2: the Barnes & Noble rule 0 (5% rebate) now excludes paypal, venmo and digital-wallet (O10, conservative), anchored on the card's wallet and third-party clauses.
- W3: GM rule 0 adds an `onstar` brand. The terms name OnStar transactions in the 7X list, next to SiriusXM. The anchor now runs through "OnStar transactions."
- W4: Barnes & Noble rule 1 (restaurants) adds `excludedBrandIds: [barnes-and-noble]`, because B&N Café purchases are excluded.
- W5: the Wyndham Plus r4 and Premier r2 notes now say that only airfare, car rental and tolls are lost. Rideshare and trains stay with the labelled transit rules.
- W6: the notes on the Gap family's outside-U.S. brand-store rules now give "outside the U.S." as the reason, not "not at retail". The rate equals the base, or the gated World rule at World levels.

## Refresh batch `barclays-refresh-2026-10` (coordinator, 2026-10-05)

- **Activation with program Terms & Conditions (general 7), 2026-10-05.** The refreshed JetBlue Plus, JetBlue Premier and Carnival pricing captures add that a cardmember assigned a new program number visits the partner site to complete the profile and that, to earn rewards, the cardmember must accept the Program Terms & Conditions. This is part of holding the loyalty account the issuer opens automatically ("holding a loyalty account is not enrollment", general 7), not a separate rewards enrollment, so the 2026-10-02 entry above stands: activation `none` on every rule of these cards. The existing `ambiguous` issue anchored on the auto-enrollment wording keeps the uncertainty visible. Frontier, whose capture has no such sentence, is unaffected.
