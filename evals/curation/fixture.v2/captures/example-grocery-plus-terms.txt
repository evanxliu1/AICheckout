Example Grocery Plus Rewards Terms

Supermarkets: merchants classified as supermarkets. Superstores, warehouse clubs, and discount stores are not supermarkets.
Online retail purchases: purchases made online from retail merchants located in the United States. Purchases of travel, gift cards, and utilities do not qualify.
Cash advances and balance transfers do not earn cash back.
Cash back is received as a statement credit.
