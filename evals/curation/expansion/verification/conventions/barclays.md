# Barclays conventions (expansion.v1)

Issuer-specific decisions for Barclays cards. They refine [general.md](general.md) and never contradict it. Seeded by the coordinator on 2026-10-02 from the verifier pass; confirmed, corrected and extended by the adjudicator on 2026-10-02.

- **Activation (general 7), confirmed and extended 2026-10-02.** Activation is `none` on every rule of a card whose terms enroll the cardholder in the partner program without a step of their own: "automatically enrolled" (Gap Inc. Encore family), "you consent to and will be enrolled" (Wyndham Rewards Earner family, Emirates Skywards, GM Rewards, Breeze), "a new … Account number will be assigned to you" (JetBlue family, Carnival, Frontier) and "are enrolled in the Upromise Program" (Upromise base rate). The anchor is the sentence part that states the enrollment, not the clipped "If the … number that you provided is found to be invalid" window.
- **Activation exceptions, 2026-10-02.** Lufthansa Miles & More stays `enroll-once`: its terms require a cardholder without a membership to enroll at miles-and-more.com to receive Miles. The Upromise 1.529% rule stays `enroll-once`: it needs a one-time link to an eligible College Savings Plan. Cards whose terms say nothing about enrollment (Luxury cards, AARP, Barnes & Noble, Capital Vacations, RCI) stay null.
- **GM Rewards (general 2), confirmed 2026-10-02.** Only the card-paid 7X counts (700); the extra 3 points GM "may" award to GM Rewards members are excluded and described in the product note.
- **Gap Inc. Encore family, confirmed and extended 2026-10-02.** Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic and Athleta share one terms capture. Labels use the terms' Mastercard-level rates (25 / 15 / 5 points per $1; payment-platform purchases 5) with an `ambiguous` issue for the World/World Elite rate (10). The product pages advertise 5X/3X/1X; the labels follow the terms and each card carries an `ambiguous` issue for the mismatch. `pointValueHundredthsOfCent` is 20 (general 14, final consistency pass 2026-10-02): Encore points are store-only points with a stated fixed value, "500 points = $1 Reward", on a co-branded card as on a store card. It replaces the earlier null under general 3. Each card also carries the `ambiguous` store-only issue anchored on "redeemable in the Encore Market … or on purchases at the Gap Inc. brands", and the value stays in the `closed-loop` product note.
- **JetBlue (general 4), confirmed 2026-10-02.** JetBlue and JetBlue Vacations purchases are separate `other` rules; TrueBlue Travel (truebluetravel.com) is `travel-portal`. The combined "3X/6X on JetBlue, JetBlue Vacations and TrueBlue Travel Purchases" heading is split into the three rules on all three cards.
- **Luxury cards point value (general 3), 2026-10-02.** Titanium 100, Black 150, Gold 200, from the terms' fixed statement-credit (and bank-deposit) redemption rate (1%, 1.5%, 2%). The 2% airfare redemption rate is travel-only and does not set the value.
- **Other point values, 2026-10-02.** RCI 100 (2,500 Rewards for a $25 statement credit). Barnes & Noble points redeem only as $25 B&N gift cards per 2,500 points: null, with a `closed-loop` product note. Under general 14 (final consistency pass 2026-10-02) the card also carries the `ambiguous` store-only issue anchored on the eGiftCard sentence; the point value stays null because gift cards are not point values. Travel-award values (Breeze "$400 value", TrueBlue pay-yourself-back) stay null.
- **Upromise, 2026-10-02.** Two `all-purchases` rules: 1.25% when no College Savings Plan is linked (125, the base) and 1.529% when linked (rateBps null, because 152.9 bps is not a whole number; the existing `out-of-scope` issue records why). The MyGiftCardsPlus gift-card benefit has no earn rate and is a merchant product note, not a rule.
- **Co-brand "up to" totals (general 2), 2026-10-02.** Frontier "up to 17x" → card 5X; Carnival "up to 6x" → card 3X; Breeze "up to 10X/4X" → card 5X/2X; the partner's share goes in the product note.
- **Issues (general 11), 2026-10-02.** Draft `missing` issues with no anchor, or anchored only on earning sentences that are already rules, redemption pointers or the merchant-coding boilerplate, are removed. The "Additional points may not be earned … mobile or digital wallet … third-party payment account" clause is boilerplate on every Barclays card and is not recorded per card.
