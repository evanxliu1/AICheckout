# U.S. Bank conventions (expansion.v1)

Issuer-specific decisions for U.S. Bank cards. They refine [general.md](general.md) and never contradict it. Seeded by the coordinator on 2026-10-02 from the verifier pass; confirmed, corrected and extended by the adjudicator on 2026-10-02.

- **Cash+ and Cash+ Secured chosen categories** (2026-10-02). They follow general 6: one rule per 5% and 2% option, activation `recurring` (the cardholder picks every quarter), the 5% options share the $2,000 combined quarterly cap then 1%, and the 2% options have no cap. The choose-two/choose-one mechanic is one `ambiguous` issue, not `out-of-scope`. The 2% "Gas stations and EV charging stations" option has two rules, `gas` and `ev-charging`. The issuer wording is the option name on the product page ("Fast food", "Department stores"), not a sample-merchants description. Shopper Cash is not a U.S. Bank card in this expansion. Final consistency pass 2026-10-02 (general 19): Fast food (5%) and Restaurants (2%) each cover only part of `dining`, and Movie theaters only part of `entertainment`; both cards carry one `ambiguous` issue for each, anchored on the sample-merchants page.
- **Edward Jones automatic top-three** (2026-10-02). General 6 with activation `none`: one rule per option for all 18 categories, at 3X, plus the 1X base and the 5X Travel Rewards Center. That is exactly the 20-rule limit, so "Electric Vehicle Charging & Gas Stations" has a single `gas` rule (no `ev-charging` copy). Options without a shared category are `other`. "Utilities & Streaming" maps to `streaming`, "Recreation" to `entertainment` and "Ground Transportation" to `transit`. There is one `ambiguous` issue saying only the top three categories earn 3X.
- **Smartly and Fidelity** (2026-10-02). Both product pages state a percentage back, so under general 1 both are `cash-back` at 200 bps, though both programs track points. Corrects the seeded bullet: a cash-back card has no point value, so Fidelity's `pointValueHundredthsOfCent` is null. Fidelity's 2% applies only when points are deposited into an eligible Fidelity account. That condition goes in an `ambiguous` issue and a product note. Smartly's balance tiers (2.5%, 3%, 4%) are product notes plus an `out-of-scope` issue, and the rule stays 200.
- **Point values** (2026-10-02). Under general 3, Harley-Davidson gift-card values (10,000 points for a $100 H-D Gift Card, $25 at H-D per 2,500 points) and Edward Jones "worth up to" values are not cash values. The point value is null, and the value is recorded in a product note. The Altitude cards' "maximum Point value" for a U.S. Bank deposit states no amount, so they stay null.
- **Harley-Davidson merchant rules** (2026-10-02). "H-D & gas stations" is split into an `other` rule for merchants classified as H-D (issuer wording names dealerships, H-D.com, the Museum, Factory Tours and H-D Insurance) and a `gas` rule. "Dining, bars, hotels & other lodging" is split into `dining` and an `other` lodging rule. Points per mile ridden (H.O.G. members) is an `out-of-scope` issue.
- **SKYPASS** (2026-10-02). Korean Air ticket purchases (Korean Air as merchant of record) are an `other` rule at the card's Korean Air multiple (2X Visa Signature, 3X Select). Every card has a 1X base rule. The Visa Signature "2X miles on travel" headline covers only Korean Air, hotels and dining, so other travel earns the base.
- **State Farm** (2026-10-02). The agent-application "up to 5%" first-year offer on insurance premiums is an `ambiguous` issue, not a rule and not a conflict, because no exact rate is stated. The standing 3% insurance rule is not limited-time. The Good Neighbor Visa states no earn rate and is dropped (general 9).
- **Travel Center rules** (2026-10-02). These are prepaid air/hotel/car bookings in the U.S. Bank Travel Center. They are `travel-portal` at the total rate (base plus the additional 4%: 5% on Cash+ cards, 4% on Shield, 5X on Altitude Connect and Edward Jones).
