# Synchrony conventions (expansion.v1)

Issuer-specific decisions for Synchrony cards. They refine [general.md](general.md) and never contradict it. Seeded by the coordinator on 2026-10-02 from the verifier pass; the adjudicator confirms, corrects or extends them.

- Store cards: rules paying only at the retailer are `other` (general 4); dollar-denominated store rewards follow general 8. Confirmed 2026-10-02. This includes Amazon.com and Whole Foods on the Amazon Store and Secured cards, Walgreens pharmacy purchases and Harbor Freight purchases.
- Rates must be stated, not derived from a point value (general 1); "$1 of rewards = 1 point" programs use the stated percentage. Confirmed 2026-10-02, extended: every Synchrony store or co-brand card whose capture states "X% back" (in rewards, points, CashPass, Harbor Freight Money and so on) is `cash-back` with `rateBps` = X × 100, even when the page also gives points per dollar. This covers Amazon Store and Secured, OnePay, TJX, JCPenney, At Home, Chico's, Soma, WHBM, J.Crew and Harbor Freight. Such cards have `pointValueHundredthsOfCent` null and no point-value anchors, since a cash-back card carries no point value (the catalog schema rejects one). The certificate or store-dollar value goes in the general 8 `ambiguous` issue.
- (2026-10-02) Store reward certificates (Club Chico's, Soma My Rewards, WHBM Prestige, J.Crew Rewards, JCPenney CashPass, TJX Rewards Certificates, At Home Rewards, Harbor Freight Money, Sam's Cash, Walgreens Cash) count as store credit under general 8: `cash-back` plus one `ambiguous` issue saying the rewards are usable only with that merchant.
- (2026-10-02) Cards that state only points per dollar (Cathay Asia Miles, Virgin Points, Sun Country points, American Eagle Real Rewards points, CareCredit Reward Points) stay `points` with `rateBps` = multiple × 100. Sun Country's "100 points = $1 in Sun Country purchases" is a travel-only value, so it is null (general 3) and goes in a product note. CareCredit keeps 100, because 1,000 points = $10 with statement credit among the options.
- Cents-per-gallon fuel cards (Phillips 66, Techron) are dropped (general 9). Confirmed 2026-10-02. MyLowe's, DICK'S and Belk are also dropped: their captures state no earn rate.
- Joining the store loyalty program before or after applying counts as `enroll-once` (general 7). Confirmed 2026-10-02 for Walgreens (myWalgreens), Chico's (Club Chico's), Soma, WHBM (Prestige) and J.Crew (Passport). Linking a Walmart+ membership to OnePay for the 5% Walmart rate is also `enroll-once`. American Eagle's Real Rewards membership is automatic once an email address is given, so it is `none`. Joining an airline program to apply (Cathay, Virgin Red) is holding a loyalty account, so activation is left null.
- (2026-10-02) Tier rates that need annual store spend (JCPenney VIP 10% after $1,000; At Home 7.5% over $350) are not rules: the base rule stays with cap null, and the threshold rate becomes an `out-of-scope` issue (general 9). JCPenney's 5% Member tier is for non-cardholders and is removed.
- (2026-10-02) First-90-days rates (OnePay 3% outside Walmart, JCPenney 5% gas and grocery) have `limitedTime.endsOn` null. The stated date is the last application date, not the end of the rate (general 10).
- (2026-10-02) Newegg's 4% is a point-of-sale discount chosen instead of financing. It is recorded as `cash-back` 400 `other` with an `ambiguous` issue. It is not store credit, so general 8 does not apply.
- (2026-10-02) Harbor Freight is not marked store-only: the captures describe use at stores and HarborFreight.com and name no card network, but never say "only". The closed-loop note states just that.
- (2026-10-02, final consistency pass) Gated rates (general 17): the Amazon Store Card 5% and Amazon Secured Card 2% rates need an eligible Prime membership, and the OnePay 5% Walmart rate needs a linked Walmart+ membership. Each card carries an `out-of-scope` issue naming the membership; the rates stay as stated. The Amazon Store Card's `ambiguous` issue anchored only on "and more" is removed (general 16). Sam's Club (Plus) and J.Crew (Passport GOLD/NAVY) already carry issues naming their conditions.


## Overlay (Stage 2 M4)

Decisions for `../../catalog-overlay.json`, made by the issuer's overlay author on 2026-10-02 under the general overlay conventions (O1–O19), then checked by an independent verifier subagent and adjudicated by the M4 coordinator (agent-verified).

- (2026-10-02) Amazon Store and Secured cards are closed loop at `amazon` and `whole-foods-market`. Amazon Pay merchants also take these cards, but Amazon Pay purchases earn nothing and the merchants can't be listed, so they aren't accepting brands. Every rate rule requires `amazon-prime` = member. A Store Card without Prime earns no percentage back, which leaves it with no rules.
- (2026-10-02) Amazon Secured accounts opened before 2021 keep the 5% benefit. That split is a gate (`amazon-secured-card-opened`) plus two added 5% rules (Amazon, Whole Foods), and both added rules also need Prime. The 2% rules carry only the Prime gate. Physical Amazon locations map to the `amazon` brand.
- (2026-10-02) Newegg and Harbor Freight are closed loop by inference. No capture says the card works "only" at the store, so O7 is not met word for word. But each card carries the store's name, names no card network and has no all-purchases rate. Newegg lists its card as a payment method apart from the networks, and Harbor Freight shows use only at its stores and HarborFreight.com. The rule: a store-named card with no network and no base rate is closed loop at that store. Open loop would also fail the base-rule invariant.
- (2026-10-02) PayPal Cashback 3% and Venmo "Pay with Venmo" 3% move to `all-purchases`, with `requiredPaymentPaths` `paypal` and `venmo`. Venmo's dining, entertainment and streaming 3% rules exclude `paypal`, `venmo` and `digital-wallet`. The terms pay 1% on third-party accounts and wallets that pass no MCC, and O10 takes the conservative reading. The PayPal send-money rule and the Venmo P2P rule are held out as peer-to-peer. Venmo's Split Bonus is noted, because it depends on friends paying the cardholder back after the purchase.
- (2026-10-02) Store-credit programs (O13): Verizon Dollars, Sam's Cash, Walgreens Cash, TJX Rewards Certificates, Club Chico's, Soma My Rewards, WHBM Prestige, J.Crew Rewards (redeemable at `j-crew` only; no capture names J.Crew Factory for redemption) and Harbor Freight Money. JCPenney CashPass and At Home Rewards stay plain `cash-back`, because no capture says where they can be spent. Amazon rewards, OnePay Points and PayPal/Venmo cash stay `cash-back` as well, since the captures allow statement credit, deposit or transfer.
- (2026-10-02) Gates (O9): `sams-club-membership` (plus/club/none). Club members earn the 1% base at Sam's Club, so only the Plus rule is gated. `walmart-plus` (member/not-member) covers OnePay's 5%. `jcpenney-rewards-tier` (insider-elite/vip) gets an added 10% VIP rule. `at-home-annual-spend` (under-350/over-350) gets an added 7.5% rule. `jcrew-passport-tier` (gold/navy/not-enrolled) has one rule per tier; cardholders not enrolled in Passport get only the base.
- (2026-10-02) Sam's Club gas and EV charging share the $6,000 yearly cap (`sharedCapId` gas-ev). The $5,000 yearly limit on Sam's Cash earned is noted (O12).
- (2026-10-02) myWalgreens: the 10% rate on Walgreens-brand products is held out as product-level, and the 5% Walgreens and Duane Reade rules stand for any Walgreens purchase. Health and wellness 3% is held out as not at retail. The 3% grocery definition names wholesale clubs, so an added `wholesale-clubs` 3% rule covers them. The myWalgreens, Chico's, Soma and WHBM bases get activation `none`, because every cardholder is enrolled by the time the account opens (O6). For myWalgreens, the terms also say Walgreens Cash is earned automatically. J.Crew's base stays `unstated`, because its capture tells cardholders who aren't in Passport to enroll.
- (2026-10-02) In the Chico's family cards, the 1% sister-brand rules are brand-scoped to the other two brands. They equal the base rate.
- (2026-10-02) OnePay's first-90-days 3% excludes `walmart` and keeps null dates (O14). The Walmart rules are brand-scoped to `walmart`.
- (2026-10-02) CareCredit: the provider-network 4% is held out (not at retail). "Health & wellness and pet store" 4% is held out with "no v3 category", since drug stores are already their own rule.
- (2026-10-02) Verizon bill payment 1% is held out (not at retail: telecom). The Verizon Shine/Loyalty extras are paid by Verizon, so they are noted (general 2).
- (2026-10-02) Co-brands are brand-scoped: Cathay + HK Express, Virgin Atlantic/Hotels/Voyages, Sun Country (only the card's 3x; the 2x for flying is airline earn), AE + Aerie (40 points per $1). Airline programs and AE Real Rewards get `redemptionBrandIds` []. The captures never say AE points are store-only.

### Verification and adjudication (2026-10-02)

The coordinator's resolution of each finding of the independent verifier subagent:
- Accepted errors: Venmo rules 0–2 now exclude `paypal`, `venmo` and `digital-wallet` (clarified O10). This settles the earlier question about the Venmo wallet clause.
- Base activation: the myWalgreens, Chico's, Soma and WHBM bases are now `none`. The anchors are the automatic-earning sentence (myWalgreens) and the enroll-before-applying sentences (the other three). J.Crew stays `unstated`.
- `jcrew-passport-tier` gains a `not-enrolled` option.
- J.Crew Rewards keeps one anchor, about redeeming in stores or online. Its redemption brands are now `j-crew` only.
- JCPenney CashPass and At Home Rewards are dropped as store programs, so both cards use plain cash back (programId null). Their certificate issues (JCPenney issue 4, At Home issue 1) are now `noted`.
- Newegg's closed-loop anchor is now the payment-method list that shows the store card apart from the networks. Newegg and Harbor Freight stay closed loop; the reasoning is in the Overlay section above.
- `amazon-prime` gate labels will be aligned at merge (coordinator).
