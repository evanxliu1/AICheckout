# Citi conventions (expansion.v1)

Issuer-specific decisions for Citi cards. They refine [general.md](general.md) and never contradict it. Seeded by the coordinator on 2026-10-02 from the verifier pass; confirmed, corrected and extended by the Citi adjudicator on 2026-10-02.

- **Strata Self-Select** follows general 6: one 3X rule per option; Select Streaming Services (the default) has activation `none`, the other four `enroll-once` (a one-time choice at citi.com/selfselect, changeable once a quarter); the draft's ambiguous issue on the default category is the one "only the selected category earns" issue. Custom Cash is not in the Citi expansion list, so no Citi card has an automatic top-spend category.
- **AAdvantage Executive**: the $150,000 calendar-year total-spend threshold that raises American Airlines purchases from 4X to 5X is not a cap (general 9): cap null plus an `out-of-scope` issue.
- **AAdvantage Hotels and Cars** (aadvantagehotels.com, aadvantagecars.com) are partner booking portals → `travel-portal` on every AAdvantage card (general 4). American Airlines purchases, and Admirals Club food and beverage paid as part of the AA purchases benefit, → `other`.
- **Citi Strata Elite Citi Nights**: the 6X Friday/Saturday 6 PM–6 AM ET restaurant rate and the 3X any-other-time rate are both kept as `dining` rules, with an `out-of-scope` issue for the time window.
- **Costco gas at Costco warehouses** pays at one merchant → `other` (general 4); other gas and EV charging keep their shared categories. The combined $7,000 cap for 5%/4% stays on each rule, with the existing `out-of-scope` issue for the shared cap.
- **Store cards that state the rate as a percentage back** (My Best Buy Visa "5% back in rewards", TSC Visa "5% in Rewards") → `cash-back` at the stated percentage (general 1), point value null, and an `ambiguous` issue that the rewards are store certificates or points (general 8 by analogy). Corrects the seeded bullet: these two cards get no point value, and the verifier's points multiples (Best Buy 2.5X → 250) and TSC point value (100) were rejected.
- **Store cards that state only points** (Macy's, Bloomingdale's, Dillard's) stay `points`. A point value is set only from the issuer's own store reward stated as a fixed amount (general 3): Macy's 1,000 points = $10 Star Money → 100; Bloomingdale's 5,000 points = $25 Reward → 50. Dillard's stays null: 1,500 points buys a choice of a 10% pass or a $10 certificate (the pass is the default), and $10 per 1,500 points is not a whole number of hundredths of a cent.
- **Store-card merchant rules** are recorded at the tier a new cardholder starts in: Macy's Silver 2X, Bloomingdale's Loyallist 3X. The higher tiers go in the relationship-tier product note and the `out-of-scope` issue.
- **Wayfair Reward Dollars and L.L.Bean Bean Bucks** follow general 8 (`cash-back` plus an `ambiguous` issue). Wayfair rules have activation `none` because cardholders are enrolled in the Wayfair Rewards Program automatically; L.L.Bean's page says nothing about enrollment, so its activation is null.
- **Captures**: the Strata Elite rewards-terms capture is a copy of the product page with the numbers blanked, and the Costco rewards-terms capture duplicates the product page. The product pages and FAQs state every rate, so both cards ship. Strata Elite has no exclusions because no terms text was captured.
- **Welcome offers** such as Best Buy's 10% on the first day of purchases (an extra 5 bonus points for new accounts) are out of scope, not `limitedTime` rules.
- **Store-only points (general 14), final consistency pass 2026-10-02.** Macy's and Bloomingdale's keep their point values (100, 50) and now carry the `ambiguous` store-only issue: Star Money is usable only at Macy's, Macy's Backstage or macys.com; Rewards only at Bloomingdale's. Dillard's already carries an `ambiguous` issue on its pass-or-certificate redemption.
- **Partial Self-Select option (general 19), final consistency pass 2026-10-02.** Strata's Live Entertainment option (live-event tickets only) maps to `entertainment` with an `ambiguous` issue anchored on the terms' definition.
