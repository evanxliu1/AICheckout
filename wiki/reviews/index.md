# Reviews

Audits and code reviews. Each page states its scope and method, lists findings with a verified/reported status and file:line anchors, gives an approach verdict, and ends with prioritised next steps (template in [how to document](../guides/how-to-document.md#templates)). Findings that get fixed are noted in `log.md` with a link back here; the review itself is not rewritten.
