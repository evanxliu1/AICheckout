# Merchant pipeline (Phase 14.3)

The merchant database ([plan](../product/phase-14-merchant-database.md)) is built from a frozen candidate list in two tiers. Tier A (the long tail) gets deterministic default records with no model. Tier B (brands, bonus-category groups, processors) goes through a small pipeline, `npm run pipeline -- merchants <command>`, that follows the [card pipeline](card-expansion-pipeline.md) pattern: a batch directory with a text-free `state.json`, claimed work packets for the two agent stages, gates on every model output, and a deterministic apply. Models never set `category`: luna proposes a candidate, Claude verifies every field, and `applyStore` applies Evan's evidence rule (`issuer`, `corroborated`, and since 2026-10-11 `definition`, [decision](../decisions/2026-10-11-definition-evidence-basis.md)). A disputed `evidence` downgrades; any other disputed field holds the whole record out; there is no adjudicator.

## Gates

- **Research** (`researchGate`): strict schema (no category, code, kind, goods flag or brand key can exist), every batch store once, the packet domain among its hosts, no public-suffix or platform host (`hostGate`), store pages on the store's hosts, lookup pages on another host and never a forum or community thread (added after the dry run; its netflix lookup predates the gate).
- **Draft** (`draftGate`): schema (at most 16 quotes since prompt 2; `supports` is `kind`, `physicalGoods`, `brand`, `category` or `definition`), quotes found verbatim (whitespace-insensitive) in `captures/<sourceId>.txt`, 25 words or fewer, brand ids from `CATALOG_V3`, source ids of that store only. Also re-run at `accept verify`.
- **Verify** (`verifyGate`): every store, all seven fields (`name`, `hosts`, `kind`, `physicalGoods`, `brandIds`, `candidateCategory`, `evidence`) decided, a reason for every dispute, since 2026-10-11 one `quotes[]` verdict per drafted quote (indices 0..n-1, `fields.evidence` disputed exactly when a quote is), `filesRead` naming every draft, sheet and capture input. `claim verify` writes `verify/catalog-lists.json` (brand ids and names, categories) and one sheet `verify/<domain>.md` per store (the drafted kind, goods flag, candidate and brands, then each drafted quote with ±300 characters of its capture), and lists issuer captures as their `*.windows.<domain>.txt` excerpts, so the verifier reads about 2 KB per store; verbatim and length checks stay mechanical (draft gate).
- **Apply** (`applyStore`, pure): disputed quotes are dropped and the rule runs on the confirmed quotes (per-quote verdicts since 2026-10-11; without them the field-level `evidence` verdict applies to every quote); when the confirmed quotes satisfy no basis the record ships on `general-merchandise`, `default`, no sources, with `downgraded: { reason }` (the disputed quotes' reasons) in `apply-report.json` and a Downgraded table in `review.md`; any other disputed field → held out. Category from the drafted `candidateCategory` only with an `issuer-terms` quote that passes `issuerQuoteEarns` (one clause names the store or its domain label, has earning wording, names the candidate category's issuer words from `ISSUER_CATEGORY_WORDS`, and has no exclusion, limit or "only" wording; curly apostrophes normalised) (`issuer`), or a `store-page` and an `mcc-lookup` quote (`corroborated`); or, since 2026-10-11 (`definitionBasis`), a `store-page` `category` quote naming the business type (`BUSINESS_TYPE_WORDS`) and no kind of store the category excludes (`CATEGORY_EXCLUDED_TYPES`: superstores, discount stores, warehouse clubs and the like for supermarkets and drugstores) plus an issuer `definition` quote whose clause names the category's issuer words with no exclusion wording (`definitionQuoteCovers`), while no sentence of the store's mined issuer windows names the store with exclusion wording about that category or its excluded kinds of store (`issuerExcludes`, conservative), and the store is a `merchant` (a `marketplace` only for `dining` under a definition with delivery wording) (`definition`, citing the store pages and the issuer page); otherwise `general-merchandise`, `default`, no sources. `issuer` wins over `corroborated` over `definition`. Review amendments 2026-10-11 ([decision](../decisions/2026-10-11-definition-evidence-basis.md#review-amendments-coordinator-2026-10-11-within-evans-rules)): an `issuer` quote may also be an inclusion list under the category ("Includes the following streaming providers: … Netflix"); a lookup quote counts only when it names the category's words and never from a forum-thread URL (`FORUM_URL`, dropped at apply); the definition's delivery wording is read on the covering clause and must be unconditional (`CONDITIONAL_WORDING`); the excluded kinds (`EXCLUDED_STORE_TYPES`, every category minus its own type, plus per-category extras) are read over the **full store-page captures**; a missing store or issuer capture refuses; an exclusion counts only within the drafted category, read by direction around "include"/"such as" (`clauseExcludes`); a refused candidate is reported with its reason (`refused` in `apply-report.json`, also beside a `downgraded` entry); cross-issuer conflicts of a granted category (`issuerConflicts`: an issuer sentence naming the store that excludes the category or lists it under another category's words) go to `conflicts` and a Conflicts table in `review.md`. Re-check amendments (2026-10-11): pharmacy words do not exclude supermarkets and grocery words do not exclude drugstores; an inclusion list needs the category before its opener and the store after it, and `CONDITIONAL_WORDING` (may, qualify, if, eligible) disqualifies an issuer quote and a delivery clause; `CALENDAR_WORDING` (months, quarters, a rate) disqualifies a definition clause; membership/members-only/club wording excludes a store from supermarkets. Sources no record cites any more leave `release-config.json`. A processor is forced to no goods, no brands, default; a processor drafted with goods or brands is held out. Record ids: the candidate's fixed id (legacy, processors), else `<label>-us` (`<label>` for processors).
- **Tier gates** (`candidates.ts`): `hostGate` (offline public-suffix and platform lists), `brandNameGate` (a tier-A label equal to a normalised catalog brand name moves to tier B), unknown brand ids and duplicated hosts refused.
