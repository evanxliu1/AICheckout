# Legacy adapter retirement: what changes for Amazon, Best Buy and Newegg (2026-10)

Phase 15.1 of the [Release A plan](../../wiki/product/phase-15-release-a.md) retires the three bundled store adapters (`amazon-us`, `best-buy-us`, `newegg-us`) and leaves the generic detector and reader (`packages/cart-reader`) as the only reader at every https store. This file is the measurement the [decision of 2026-10-19](../../wiki/decisions/2026-10-19-retire-legacy-adapters.md) asks for: what each reader showed on the same pages, before deletion. **It is a record, not a gate**; Evan directed the retirement unconditionally.

Both tables are text-free: a page is named by its fixture or capture id and URL, and a row carries only the readers' outputs (kind, amount, currency, or a reason code); part 2 adds the expected amount with its summary row's label ("Subtotal", "Total"). No other page text, no selectors or product names.

How to read a row. **Adapter** is the legacy reader's output on the adapter's own cart URL (`readCheckoutPage`): an amount of a kind, or an unavailable reason. **Generic** is what the extension shows at that page after retirement, derived from `readCartPage(document, { url })` the way `manual-reader.ts:readAnyCart` maps it: `amount` (the badge and popup show the dollar estimate), `rates view` (the page is a cart or checkout but the amount was not read with certainty: cards compared by rate, never a wrong amount), or `hidden` (not a cart, an empty cart or a non-USD cart: no badge). The detector's verdict and reason, and the reader's reason, are the reason codes.

**What changes for these stores after retirement**

- **Best Buy and Newegg: nothing visible.** On all four cart pages the generic reader shows the same kind and the same amount as the adapter (Best Buy the order total, Newegg the selected subtotal), and both empty carts stay hidden (detector `none`). The A4 condition is met on these two stores.
- **Amazon: the dollar estimate becomes the rates view.** The generic reader withholds with `credit-unclear` on both cart pages. `explainCart` shows why: the summary's own subtotal row is read correctly (4349 / 8698, `inSummary`), but a card-offer panel outside the summary contributes a row the reader classes as a `credit` (a "savings" line), and the reader withholds whenever a credit row exists and the chosen kind is not after-credit. The badge still appears (reason `withheld` is a rates-view reason), so the shopper sees which card earns most at Amazon, without the cents. Never a wrong amount.
- **Empty carts match on all three stores.** The adapter's `empty-cart` and the detector's `none` both hide the badge; the generic reader's withhold reasons on those pages (`credit-unclear`, `total-outside-summary`, `no-summary`) never reach the badge because the detector stops first (`not-a-cart`).
- **Possible reader follow-up, not a gate:** confining credit rows to the summary block (the way `inSummary` already scopes the chosen row) would turn Amazon's two withholds into shown subtotals; it is a generic reader change, measured on the development split only, not a store config. It is planned as 15.1b in the [Release A plan](../../wiki/product/phase-15-release-a.md#progress).
- **Caveats.** One capture per state, one read each, no timing; Best Buy `/checkout/...` steps (adapter-matched, never captured) are not measured; the Amazon captures were taken at `/cart` (the adapter also matched `/gp/cart/view.html`, which was not visited).
