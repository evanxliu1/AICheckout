# Rewards core

**Catalog schema 3** (`catalogV3Schema`, `CATALOG_V3`, version `2026-10-02.expansion.1`) is the catalog the extension bundles: 178 cards of the ten largest U.S. issuers, with reward programs and their values (cash, a published estimate, an issuer-stated value, or none), brands for merchant-scoped rules and closed-loop store cards, cardholder gates (memberships, tiers), chosen and automatic categories, required and excluded payment paths (including Venmo) and start/end dates. `CATALOG_V3` is generated by `npm run catalog:v3` from the verified corpora and the overlay in `evals/curation/expansion/`; `npm run catalog:v3:check` fails when it is stale. The v3 engine (`src/engine-v3.ts`, `compareV3`) ranks valued cards by guaranteed cash-equivalent minimum and unvalued programs after them in units. `catalogSchema` accepts schemas 1, 2 and 3 by `schemaVersion`. Details: [rewards engine](../../wiki/system/rewards-engine.md).

**Catalog schema 2** (`catalogV2Schema`, `CATALOG_V2`, 7 cards, published as hosted release 1) is the release 1 catalog: merchant profiles (online retail, physical goods, U.S., expected category, predicted MCC with confidence and sources) and every earning rule a card's terms state (category, issuer wording, total rate, paid-on-payment part, cap as `none` / `spend` with period and after-cap rate / `unstated`, activation, U.S.-only, excluded payment paths, limited time). Cached v1 and v2 releases stay readable. `CATALOG_V2` is generated by `npm run catalog:v2` from the verified gold labels in `evals/curation/real/`, never from model output; `npm run catalog:v2:check` fails when it is stale. The v2 engine (`src/engine-v2.ts`) applies `all-purchases` always, `online-retail` only at online physical-goods merchants, MCC-group rules only when the merchant's expected category matches, and portal or `other` rules never; uncertain conditions (unconfirmed category, unconfirmed enroll-once or recurring activation, non-card payment path, unstated cap, unknown cap usage) produce a range from the base to the bonus. Activation `unstated` (not mentioned by the issuer) is treated as no activation, and no card is ever estimated below its base. The notes below describe schema 1.

Pure TypeScript domain logic shared by the extension, the API, the review app and the curation tools, consumed as TypeScript source. No browser, network, database, model, or clock access. Callers supply the catalog, owned cards, purchase facts, and current time.

Money is integer US cents; rates use basis points. Exact integer products are summed before truncation to cents. Results are purchase estimates, not a reproduction of issuer billing-period rounding. Only one non-stacking online retail bonus is supported; unsupported rule shapes fail closed.

Unknown annual usage, activation, and category eligibility create ranges. Ranking uses the lower estimate, with the user's default card as a tie-breaker. The response flags when a competing card could do better. Usage recorded on another date or in another year becomes unknown, not zero. The app does not track intervening transactions. A missing owned card in a changed catalog blocks comparison rather than silently narrowing the wallet.

The `2026-09-25.pilot.2` snapshot (`PILOT_CATALOG`, no longer bundled in the extension; kept for v1 tests) was an engineering seed whose issuer terms were checked September 25, 2026 and expire after 30 days under our maintenance policy. Adding Newegg did not change source or expiry dates. Issuer terms can change earlier. It covers eligible Best Buy US and Newegg US retail purchases only. Travel/entertainment bonuses, sign-up offers, account-specific offers, financing, fees, and other purchase categories are outside this snapshot. Previously published single-merchant snapshots remain valid with their original scope.

Sources: [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/learn-grow/money-management/quicksilver-card-benefits/), [American Express reward terms](https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/terms/blue-cash-everyday), and [category guidance](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/rewards-info/retail.htm). American Express's category depends on merchant-supplied transaction data; a retailer domain cannot prove how a payment will post. Both merchants' eligibility is a user-confirmed pilot assumption, not verified issuer coding. Browser cart observations do not verify how a transaction will be rewarded.
