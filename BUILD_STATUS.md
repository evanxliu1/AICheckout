## Outcome

A verified Chrome Web Store release candidate for US/USD checkout comparisons, plus a demonstrable full-stack and LLM catalog-maintenance system. Recommendations compare cards already owned. AI drafts source-grounded catalog changes; reviewed, versioned rules drive deterministic calculations. No card numbers or bank credentials.

Use $0 spending until a budget is supplied. Preserve the historical `audit/` and existing workspace changes. No fabricated usage, savings, accuracy, live retailer validation, CI, or deployment claims.
